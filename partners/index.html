Partner With The Committed

Where capital meets conviction.

The Committed brings together accredited women investors at a pivotal moment, as they transition from angel investing to becoming Limited Partners in venture capital funds. This is the infrastructure for women claiming their place in the investor class during the largest intergenerational wealth transfer in history.

The community

The Community

Our members are decision-makers, aged 40 to 55, with both investable assets and consumer power. They are:

  • Making their first, or next, fund commitments
  • Inheriting, generating, or stewarding significant wealth
  • Building conviction around capital deployment
  • Seeking peer community at the intersection of education and action

They don't need to be sold. They need to be met where they are.

The moment

Why This Matters Now

Women are projected to control $30 trillion in investable assets by 2030. The Committed provides structured access to this emerging cohort of sophisticated investors and high-net-worth consumers, not as an audience to extract from, but as a community to align with.
