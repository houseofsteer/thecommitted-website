The Opportunity

The Committed serves accredited women investors, aged 40 to 55, with $1M+ in investable assets, at a pivotal financial moment. They are:

  • Making their first fund commitments
  • Inheriting or managing significant family wealth
  • Building systematic investment frameworks
  • Seeking trusted advisors and service providers

This is your qualified prospect list, actively seeking expertise.

Why Service Partners Invest

Our members are in transition. They're building new relationships with:

  • Wealth advisors and financial planners
  • Estate planning attorneys and tax strategists
  • Family office service providers
  • Private bankers and lending institutions
  • Concierge services, including property management, travel, and lifestyle

Who do other sophisticated investors trust?

Your partnership positions you as the answer.

The Value Proposition

You're not buying an ad. You're buying trust.

Our members don't respond to cold outreach or generic marketing. They respond to:

  • Peer recommendations
  • Educational thought leadership
  • Curated introductions from trusted sources
  • Association with platforms they respect

Service partnership gives you all four.

Partnership Opportunities

Educational Sponsorship

Support our cohort programming and position your expertise through:

  • Guest expert sessions. Host workshops on estate planning, tax strategy, portfolio construction, or other relevant topics
  • Resource contributions. Provide frameworks, templates, or guides that become part of member curriculum
  • Office hours. Offer limited private consultations to members, which is qualified lead generation

Summit & Event Presence

Relationship-building at our marquee gatherings through:

  • Speaking opportunities. Keynote, panel, or breakout session at annual summit
  • VIP networking access. Private dinners, hosted conversations with members
  • Sponsor recognition. Discreet acknowledgment as education supporter

Curated Introductions

Strategic positioning within the community:

  • Trusted partner designation. Listed on members-only resources page
  • Targeted introductions. Warm referrals to members seeking your specific services
  • Member communications. Feature in newsletter or member updates as vetted resource
